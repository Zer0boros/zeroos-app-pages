Privacy Policy — zeroOS for iPhone

Last updated: Monday, 9 November 2026 (CET)

Who this is for

zeroOS for iPhone ("the app") is published as a companion to a zeroOS server that you or your organization run on a Mac. The app is not a standalone AI chatbot.

Data the app handles

Messages and chat

Text you send and receive in chat is processed on your Mac over a private network built into the app between this iPhone and that Mac. The product path for the store build keeps that built-in private network + device-key design. (Open-source networking components are credited in the in-app Acknowledgements screen when that build links them.)

Library and Imagine

When Library or Imagine is available on this phone, Library and Imagine content and routes go to your Mac over the same private connection used for chat. Library documents are stored for your account on that Mac. Imagine prompts and images are generated by the Mac’s local image runtime and do not leave the Mac; images are stored for your account on that Mac. The phone does not keep a separate on-disk Library or Imagine cache; opened items stay in memory for the current screen and are fetched again from the Mac if you return. The phone does not open a separate third-party Library or Imagine host. Stronger App Store privacy-label statements wait until a fuller data-flow review is done.

Device key and enrollment

Each iPhone uses its own device key. Enrollment starts from an invitation created on the Mac. Sign-in does not finish until the device is approved for that server.

Face ID / device passcode

When the app asks you to confirm with Face ID, Touch ID, or your device passcode, that check happens on the iPhone. zeroOS does not receive your biometric templates.

Saved servers on the phone

The app may store server connection details, credentials needed to reach your Mac, and cached data on this iPhone so you can reconnect. Removing a server deletes that phone-side material for that server. It does not delete chats stored on the Mac.

Sign out, Remove server, and account deletion

  • Sign out clears this server's sign-in on this iPhone. On the device-key product path it is local-only (it does not call the Mac to delete the account). It is not account deletion.
  • Remove server removes saved connection, credentials, and cached data for that server from this iPhone. Chats on the Mac are not deleted. Server-side access may still exist until an admin revokes it. Remove server does not call block, revoke-device, or delete-account on the Mac.
  • Account deletion from the iPhone app is not available in the current build. There is no self-service delete. Soft-block or revoke on the Mac does not by itself erase that person’s stored data on the server. Contact the person who runs your zeroOS Mac. Providing a clear in-app deletion path (or an equally clear path that App Review accepts) is an open App Store requirement and is not finished yet.

Advertising and tracking

The app is not designed to show ads or to include third-party advertising trackers. Broader statements about analytics, crash reporting, or that nothing leaves your Mac or private network wait until logs and diagnostics are confirmed.

Logs and diagnostics

Whether crash logs or diagnostics are collected by the app or the Mac server — and where they go — is not yet confirmed. This page will be updated when that is known.

Children

The app is intended for people who operate or are invited to a private Mac server. It is not directed at children.

Contact

For server or access issues, contact the person who runs your zeroOS Mac. For product privacy questions about the iPhone app listing, contact zig4samura@gmail.com.

Changes

We may update this policy. The last-updated date at the top will change when the published text changes.
