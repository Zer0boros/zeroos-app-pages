About — zeroOS for iPhone

Last updated: Monday, 9 November 2026 (CET)

zeroOS for iPhone is a companion app for a zeroOS server that you (or someone you trust) run on a Mac. It is not a standalone chatbot. Without that Mac server, there is nothing for the phone to connect to.

The app uses a private network built into the app and a device key for this iPhone. Sign-in does not finish until the device is approved for that server.

Read the Privacy Policy and Support pages for details. For product questions about the iPhone app listing, contact zig4samura@gmail.com. For server or access issues, contact the person who runs your zeroOS Mac.
